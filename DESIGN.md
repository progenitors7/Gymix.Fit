# Gymix visual direction

Landing page mode: Persuade. Approved concept: gym-owner daily workflow with a scroll-driven member-renewal story, interactive dashboard, member-count calculation, pricing and setup.

Use warm neutral #faf9f6, ink #22212b, muted #686571, violet #7c3aed, Inter. Functional green means collected/active. Keep borders fine, corners proportional and spacing deliberate. No neon, fabricated social proof or decorative sparkles.

Desktop: editorial split hero with gym photography; three narrative steps next to a sticky illustrative product dashboard. IntersectionObserver advances the demonstration without intercepting scrolling. Mobile: normal document scrolling, explicit step buttons, stacked content and fixed bottom actions. Honor reduced motion. Scope landing styles under gx classes to preserve app screens.

Pricing retained from prior landing: ₹599 / month; ₹1,499 / three months; ₹4,999 / thirteen months. Final billing remains authoritative. All signup links use owner registration. Demo interactions never write database records.

## Motion revision

User requested a more immersive scrolling experience and matching auth. A photographic pinned hero uses coordinated image cropping, title movement and pointer-following membership pass. Scroll reveals a product-focused typographic statement, then moves a three-panel feature track horizontally on desktop. Mobile uses stacked panels. Framer Motion values drive transforms without per-frame React renders; reduced motion keeps content readable without spatial effects. Auth uses the same photography, violet, neutral canvas and typography, with forms in normal document flow. No simulated platform usage statistics.

## 3D direction (supersedes photographic hero)

The user rejected the photographic motion direction and requested visible 3D. The hero now uses a CSS 3D product assembly: perspective camera, a dashboard chassis, member pass, payment receipt and attendance panel on independent Z planes. Scroll opens the assembly and returns it to a frontal view; pointer input adds spring-driven camera rotation. An ink #100e16 stage with muted lilac #b99bdc surfaces and fine technical lines replaces the photographic hero. Auth adopts the same 3D product scene, dark canvas, lilac controls and dimensional logo. The shared scene is scoped to Marketing/GymixScene; no heavy 3D runtime or perpetual render loop. Mobile scales the assembly and preserves native scrolling. Reduced motion shows a stable composition. Keep all sample figures labelled illustrative demo.

## Full-page chapters and pricing

Extended the accepted 3D hero identity through the public landing: dark product demo, scroll-linked perspective on dashboard/calculator, a continuous setup timeline, a lilac pricing chapter, dark FAQs and a dimensional closing brand slab. All motion is tied to section progress, rather than autoplay or repeated reveal delays. Mobile renders stable stacked compositions; reduced motion keeps every section usable.

Pricing persuasion uses honest comparison: show low upfront monthly option, rounded monthly equivalents, exact upfront duration totals, actual savings against ₹599 monthly payments, and a shared member-count cost calculator. Annual remains ₹4,999 for 13 months (₹384.54 unrounded/month); quarterly ₹1,499 for 3 months; monthly ₹599. Daily annual equivalent rounds to ₹13 using 30-day months, with its upfront condition adjacent. No fabricated original prices, popularity claims, urgency or hidden instalment assumptions. Feature scope is identical across durations.

## Readability refinement

User reported cropped feature content and blurred text. Replaced the oversized horizontal track with a bounded sticky image stage and full-width narrative steps; feature controls switch the image and scroll to the corresponding step on desktop. Mobile keeps normal flow and scene buttons do not jump the page. Text is never rotated or scaled on pricing, the calculator or the readable product demo. Pricing motion is an independent accent rule; depth is confined to decorative frames and images. Increased billing, disclosures and feature paragraph sizes. The intentionally dimensional hero and decorative closing brand remain 3D.
