# Gymix product

Gymix helps independent single-branch gym owners manage members, membership plans, payments, attendance and member connection requests. Primary audience: local gyms with 50–300 members. The public landing page should help an owner understand the daily workflow and start owner registration.

Do not invent customers, testimonials, performance guarantees or revenue claims. WhatsApp automation is under improvement and biometric integration has not been verified with hardware. Public demos use clearly marked fictional data.
